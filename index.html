RiskyID Screening & Monitoring

RiskyID is a specialized platform for screening and monitoring individuals and entities against global risk information, helping organizations identify potentially high-risk relationships and maintain greater visibility throughout the lifecycle of those relationships.

  • Individual and entity screening
  • Sanctions and official list screening
  • Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
  • High-risk individual and entity profiles
  • Ongoing monitoring
  • Bulk screening
  • Structured screening reports
  • API integration capabilities

RiskyID leverages Dow Jones Risk & Compliance data as part of its screening capabilities.

Plus Comply Compliance Training

Plus Comply is a digital training platform focused on compliance, financial crime prevention, risk management, corporate governance, and related professional development topics. It provides organizations with structured online training that can support corporate compliance and professional development programs.

  • AML / CFT
  • Financial crime prevention
  • Risk management
  • Anti-corruption
  • Sanctions
  • Corporate governance
  • Cybersecurity
  • Data protection & regulatory compliance
